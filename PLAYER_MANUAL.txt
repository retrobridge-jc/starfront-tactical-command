STARFRONT: TACTICAL COMMAND
THE 1984 EDITION - PLAYER HANDBOOK
BY JOHN CHIRILLO

A FRONTIER COMMAND TRANSMISSION
YEAR 2387. The Vanguard patrols a six-by-six sector of the frontier.
Array warships threaten colonies and vital supply routes. Your orders:
protect the frontier, complete assignments, and bring your ship home.

LOADING FROM THE 1541 DISK
Insert STARFRONT.D64 into drive 8 in VICE, or write the image to a
compatible physical or modern C64 disk device. At the READY prompt:
  LOAD "STARFRONT",8
  RUN
The disk contains a standard BASIC PRG file, not a machine-code loader.
Alternatively load STARFRONT.PRG directly in VICE or on a C64.

TITLE SCREEN
Original SID music plays while the title screen is displayed.
Press M to mute or restore the theme; press any other key to start.

COMMAND BRIDGE
1 MAP      Survey the 6x6 frontier
2 WARP     Move N/S/W/E (8 energy, 1 day)
3 SCAN     Inspect nearby sectors (3 energy)
4 BATTLE   Engage enemies in your sector
5 DOCK     Resupply at a colony (2 days)
6 STATUS   Review ship and mission status
7 HELP     Read onboard instructions
8 SHIP     Crew reports and Captain's Log
9 REPAIR   Emergency damage control

TACTICAL CONSOLE
1 BEAM: 12 energy per shot
2 TORPEDO: expend one torpedo
3 BRACE: defensive action, costs 5 energy
4 DISENGAGE: attempt to leave combat
5 TARGET SCAN: inspect hostile condition
6 AIM SYSTEM: target hull, weapons, engines or shields

GALAXY KEY
V Vanguard; M mission; C colony; ! distress; ? unexplored.
Numbers mark hostile contacts in explored sectors.

FRONTIER COMMAND MISSIONS
RECON: reach the objective and scan.
INTERCEPT: destroy the hostile contacts at the objective.
DELIVERY: reach the assigned colony and dock.
Watch mission deadlines. Rewards and penalties affect your campaign.

THE VANGUARD CREW
Command 8 provides Engineering, Navigation, Tactical and Captain's
Recommendation reports. The Captain's Log holds six recent events.
Consulting reports costs no energy or days.

CAPTAIN'S NOTES
Save energy for emergencies. Repair disabled engines before warping
and damaged sensors before reconnaissance. Torpedoes are precious.
Dock whenever possible to replenish your ship. A campaign is won
by defeating the Array fleet before the 45-day limit.

HISTORICAL NOTE
STARFRONT was lost in 1984, found and revamped and released in 2026.
The software uses original C64-era BASIC V2, PETSCII and SID methods.
VICE playtesting passed all ten RC1 checks; physical 1984-era hardware
compatibility has not been independently confirmed.
